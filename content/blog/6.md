It is a time for celebration! Today, we are proud to announce that the Certiflo platform has successfully generated and secured its **100,000th digital certificate**.

This milestone is not just a number—it represents 100,000 achievements recognized, careers advanced, and moments celebrated. From coding bootcamps in San Francisco to medical training centers in London, Certiflo is becoming the global standard for trust.

## The Journey So Far

*   **Year 1**: We started with a simple PDF generator.
*   **Year 2**: We introduced Blockchain verification.
*   **Year 3 (Now)**: We are processing thousands of credentials daily for enterprise clients.

## Thank You to Our Community

We could not have done this without our incredible user base. Your feedback on our API, our templates, and our verification UI has shaped every line of code we write.

100k is just the beginning. Our roadmap for 2026 includes:
1.  AI-generated certificate designs.
2.  Integration with Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.
3.  Even faster verification speeds.

Here is to the next million! 🥂
